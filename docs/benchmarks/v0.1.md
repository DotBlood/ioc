## Graph Traversal — `internal/graph/bench_test.go`

### Setup
- Chain: 100K nodes, linear Chain (node0→node1→...→node99999), 99999 edges
- Star: 100K nodes, 1 root with 99999 children, 99999 edges
- 10K chain variants for DFS depth-limited tests

### Baseline (GOMAXPROCS=1)

| Benchmark | Iter | ns/op | B/op | allocs/op |
|-----------|------|-------|------|-----------|
| `BFS/Chain_100K` | 20-24× | ~56,854,838 | 16,872,088 | 100,556 |
| `BFS/Star_100K` | 33-44× | ~31,990,000 | 22,614,936 | 585 |
| `DFS/Unlimited_10000` | 369-408× | ~3,080,182 | 1,276,776 | 98 |
| `DFS/Depth100_10000` | 45-50K× | ~24,958 | 8,536 | 17 |
| `BFS/Unlimited_10000` | 334-372× | ~3,449,283 | 1,596,696 | 10,095 |

### Multi-core (default GOMAXPROCS=12)

| Benchmark | Iter | ns/op | vs GOMAXPROCS=1 |
|-----------|------|-------|-----------------|
| `BFS/Chain_100K` | 13-14× | ~82,447,107 | 1.45× slower |
| `BFS/Star_100K` | 34-37× | ~33,898,658 | 1.06× slower |
| `DFS/Unlimited_10000` | 374-436× | ~3,434,425 | 1.11× slower |
| `DFS/Depth100_10000` | 51-55K× | ~21,912 | 0.88× faster |
| `BFS/Unlimited_10000` | 363-391× | ~2,890,234 | 0.84× faster |

**Analysis:** BFS/Chain is ~45% slower under multi-core due to memory bandwidth contention on 100K visited map allocations. Single-threaded execution is optimal for graph traversal in v0.1.

## Embedding — `internal/embedding/bench_test.go`

### Setup
- `MockEmbedder` with `DefaultMockConfig()` (384 dims)
- 100 distinct texts, ~80 chars each
- Mock embedder is trivial (FNV-1a + splitmix64), not representative of real model inference

### Baseline (GOMAXPROCS=1)

| Benchmark | Iter | ns/op | B/op | allocs/op |
|-----------|------|-------|------|-----------|
| `MockEmbedder_100Texts` | 6-8K× | ~184,481 | 157,136 | 102 |

### Multi-core (default GOMAXPROCS=12)

| Benchmark | Iter | ns/op | B/op | allocs/op |
|-----------|------|-------|------|-----------|
| `MockEmbedder_100Texts` | 5.5-7.4K× | ~186,157 | 157,138 | 102 |

**Analysis:** Near-identical performance across core counts — MockEmbedder is CPU-bound on a single goroutine and too fast for parallelism to help. Real model inference will be orders of magnitude slower and benefit from GPU/batching.

## Retrieval — `internal/retrieval/bench_test.go`

### Setup
- **Coarse:** `BruteForceIndex` with 10K entries, 384 dims, deterministic RNG
- **Full:** 1000 candidates, 384 dims, BM25 text index, RRF fusion (K=60)
  - `Full`: Engine.Query with embedder + vector + text + fusion
  - `Full_NoEmbed`: pre-embedded query vector, skip embedder
  - `OnlyVector`: vector search only

### Baseline (GOMAXPROCS=1)

| Benchmark | Iter | ns/op | B/op | allocs/op |
|-----------|------|-------|------|-----------|
| `Coarse_10KVectors` | 180-307× | ~5,292,807 | 1,584 | 30 |
| `Full_1000Candidates/Full` | 1.7-2K× | ~605,800 | 67,896 | 462 |
| `Full_1000Candidates/Full_NoEmbed` | 1.7-2.1K× | ~596,002 | 66,232 | 458 |
| `Full_1000Candidates/OnlyVector` | 2.9-3.7K× | ~333,424 | 6,848 | 112 |

### Multi-core (default GOMAXPROCS=12)

| Benchmark | Iter | ns/op | B/op | allocs/op |
|-----------|------|-------|------|-----------|
| `Coarse_10KVectors` | 216-250× | ~5,426,703 | 1,584 | 30 |
| `Full/Full` | 1.8-2K× | ~629,023 | 67,896 | 462 |
| `Full/Full_NoEmbed` | 1.9-2K× | ~577,239 | 66,232 | 458 |
| `Full/OnlyVector` | 2.8-3.4K× | ~393,888 | 6,848 | 112 |

**Analysis:**
- BruteForce 10K: steady ~5.3µs per query, 30 allocs, 1.5KB — pure O(N×dim) with no SIMD in v0.1
- Embedding cost (Full vs Full_NoEmbed) is negligible (~10µs) with MockEmbedder — will dominate with real embedder
- Fusion overhead (Full_NoEmbed vs OnlyVector) is ~260µs due to rank map construction and merge at 1000 candidates
- Retrieval is single-goroutine serial in v0.1 — no multi-core scaling expected

## CAS Store — `internal/store/bench_test.go`

### Setup
- Semi-compressible data: 4 distinct paragraphs (A/B/C/D) repeated cyclically
- `Blob_1MB`: 1,048,576 bytes → zstd compression + CDC chunking
- `Blob_1KB`: 1024 bytes → overhead of small objects
- `Open_1MB`: read stored blob back via `io.Copy(io.Discard)`

### Baseline (GOMAXPROCS=1)

| Benchmark | Iter | ns/op | MB/s | B/op | allocs/op |
|-----------|------|-------|------|------|-----------|
| `Store/Blob_1MB` | 994-1262× | ~995,033 | ~1,073 | 2,244,415 | 31 |
| `Store/Blob_1KB` | 35-55K× | ~23,875 | ~42 | 3,055 | 11 |
| `Open_1MB` | 1071-1281× | ~1,002,322 | ~1,046 | 1,355,306 | 32 |

### Multi-core (default GOMAXPROCS=12)

| Benchmark | Iter | ns/op | MB/s | B/op | allocs/op |
|-----------|------|-------|------|------|-----------|
| `Store/Blob_1MB` | 1128-1204× | ~976,783 | ~1,073 | 2,256,018 | 31 |
| `Store/Blob_1KB` | 47-53K× | ~23,327 | ~43 | 3,323 | 11 |
| `Open_1MB` | 1006-1275× | ~967,397 | ~1,083 | 1,359,414 | 39 |

**Analysis:**
- 1MB throughput: ~1GB/s for both store and open — zstd + chunking is fast enough that CAS is not a bottleneck
- 1KB throughput: ~43MB/s — per-operation overhead dominates small objects
- Allocation: 1MB store allocates ~2.2MB per operation (compression buffers + chunk manifests + zstd overhead), 1KB store allocates ~3KB
- Near-perfect speed consistency across core counts — CAS I/O is single-goroutine sequential

## Summary

### Hot paths and regression baselines

| Component | Key metric | v0.1 baseline (GOMAXPROCS=1) |
|-----------|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Graph BFS | 100K chain traversal | 57ms, 16.9MB, 100K allocs |
| Graph BFS | 100K star traversal | 32ms, 22.6MB, 585 allocs |
| Graph DFS | 10K unlimited | 3.1ms, 1.3MB, 98 allocs |
| Retrieval | BruteForce 10K×384 | 5.3µs, 1.6KB, 30 allocs |
| Retrieval | Full pipeline 1K | 605µs, 68KB, 462 allocs |
| Embedding | Mock 100 texts | 184µs, 157KB, 102 allocs |
| CAS Store | 1MB | 1.0ms, 2.2MB, 31 allocs |
| CAS Open | 1MB | 1.0ms, 1.4MB, 32 allocs |

### Notes
- **Mock embedder is not realistic** — real model inference will dominate the Full pipeline cost. The Full_NoEmbed sub-benchmark provides a baseline for the non-embedding portion.
- **Graph traversal is memory-bound** — BFS/Chain 100K allocates 16.9MB per traversal (visited map + queue). Future optimizations (bitset visited, slice pool) will primarily reduce allocation, not latency.
- **BruteForce is O(N×dim)** — future vector indexes (HNSW, IVF) will reduce search to O(log N). Baseline is for regression.
- **CAS throughput (~1GB/s) is sufficient** — ingestion pipeline will be bottlecked by bbolt, not CAS.
- **No shared mutable fixtures** — each benchmark function creates its own test data. Deterministic RNG (`rand.NewPCG(1,2)`) ensures reproducibility.
- **No `require.*` in hot path** — assertions use `b.Fatal`/`b.Fatalf` only, avoiding testify overhead in measurement.
